We handle registration for you

Most contractors leave registration as the customer's homework — and most customers forget, quietly cutting their coverage in half. Mechanical Temp registers your equipment with Goodman on your behalf within days of installation, and you receive confirmation with your closing paperwork. Model and serial numbers stay on file in our system, so a warranty question years from now is one phone call.

What the Parts Warranty Covers

  • Functional parts that fail due to manufacturer defect
  • Replacement part provided at no charge through Goodman
  • Coverage stays with your registered equipment

Good to Know

  • Labor to diagnose and install parts is not included — that's standard across the HVAC industry, every brand
  • Routine maintenance items (filters, etc.) aren't warranty parts
