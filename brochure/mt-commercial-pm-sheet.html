What downtime actually costs a business

Lost sales hours

A comfort failure on a 90° Saturday doesn't wait for Monday. Customers leave, product suffers, staff struggles — and emergency service is always the most expensive kind.

Sensitive inventory & compliance

Dispensaries, food service, and inventory-heavy retail live and die by temperature and humidity. Consistent conditions aren't comfort — they're product protection and, in some industries, compliance.

Equipment that runs 10× harder

Commercial rooftop units run long hours in Michigan extremes. Neglected units fail years early — and commercial factory warranties are far shorter than most owners assume (typically 1-year parts).

What every PM visit includes

  • Replace/service all filters
  • Clean condenser & evaporator coils as needed
  • Inspect & test electrical connections, contactors, capacitors
  • Check refrigerant pressures & temperatures
  • Inspect belts, motors, and bearings; adjust/lubricate
  • Test heating operation, ignition, and safety controls
  • Clear and treat condensate drains
  • Verify thermostat/controls operation & setpoints
  • Inspect heat exchanger (heating season)
  • Written service report with findings & recommendations, every visit
