Installation Warranty — Parts & Labor

Residential & Light Commercial Equipment Installations

Every Mechanical Temp installation is backed in writing — both the equipment and the workmanship. Your manufacturer covers the parts. We cover the labor. Here is exactly what that means:

10-YEAR PARTS

Manufacturer's limited parts warranty — registered by us, for you. We register your equipment with the manufacturer within days of installation, securing the full 10-year parts term (unregistered equipment typically receives only 5 years). You'll receive registration confirmation with your closing paperwork.

2Years Labor

Standard — Included

Every installation includes a 2-year workmanship & labor warranty at no charge. If a covered component fails, our labor to diagnose and replace it costs you nothing.

Included with every install
5Years Labor

Maintenance Plan Members

Keep your system on our annual maintenance plan and your labor warranty extends to 5 years — active for as long as your plan stays current. Proper maintenance also protects your manufacturer parts coverage.

Included with active maintenance plan
10Years Labor

Total Coverage Option

Match your labor coverage to the full 10-year parts term with an extended labor warranty policy — one price at installation, a decade of covered labor, transferable with the home.

Optional — priced on your quote

What Labor Coverage Includes

  • Diagnosis and replacement labor for covered warranty parts
  • Repair of installation workmanship — connections, brazing, wiring, condensate, and mounting
  • Refrigerant recovered/recharged when required by a covered repair
  • No trip charge on covered warranty calls

Not Covered

  • Damage from accident, neglect, flooding, fire, power surges, or acts of nature
  • Repairs made by others, or alterations to the installed system
  • Maintenance items: filters, cleaning, drain clearing, and cosmetic issues
  • Systems with a lapsed maintenance plan (5-year tier) or unregistered equipment
Labor warranty applies to the original installation address and equipment installed by Mechanical Temp LLC. Manufacturer parts warranty terms are set by the equipment manufacturer and require registration; Mechanical Temp completes registration on the customer's behalf. The 5-year tier remains active while the annual maintenance plan is current and lapses if the plan is cancelled. The 10-year option is provided through an extended labor warranty policy; full policy terms provided at purchase. This document summarizes coverage — the signed warranty certificate issued with your installation governs.
