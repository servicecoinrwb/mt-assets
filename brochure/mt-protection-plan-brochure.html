Straight answers

"Why join the plan if the first 2 years of labor are free?"

Because the plan was never about the labor warranty — it's the maintenance. Two professional tune-ups a year would cost $250–300 on their own, and that documented maintenance is what keeps your 10-year parts warranty enforceable from day one. The extra labor years are our thank-you on top.

"What's the difference between the 5-year and the 10-year?"

The 5-year rides along with your plan membership. The 10-year is bought once and it's yours no matter what — insured, no deductibles, transferable. Years 6–10 are when major components actually tend to fail, and that's exactly what it covers.

"What if something breaks and I'm covered?"

You call, we come, we fix it — no labor bill, no trip charge on covered repairs. One number, one company, the same people who installed it. No 1-800 runaround.

No. The plan is month to month — cancel anytime. (The 5-year labor extension stays active only while your plan is, which is the honest trade.)
